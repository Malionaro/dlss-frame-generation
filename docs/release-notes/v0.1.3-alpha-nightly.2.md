## Vulkan Frame Generation nightly 2 (after 0.1.2-alpha)

Untested nightly build for **Minecraft 26.3** (Fabric, Vulkan backend). Expect bugs; back up your worlds. The JAR still carries the version `0.1.2-alpha` in its name.

### New: full RT lighting

Inside the RT range the world is now lit by ray tracing instead of darkening vanilla's lighting:

- Block textures as albedo, lit by the sun by day and the moon by night, with soft shadows.
- Sky light with one light bounce (indirect light), block lights (torches, lanterns, lava) with shadows, glowing blocks.
- Entities cast and receive RT shadows.
- Reflections on water, ice, metal, glass and wet ground, with sun and moon highlights.
- Refraction through water and glass; water with animated waves, depth absorption and Fresnel.
- Analytic sky (sunrise, sunset, night, rain), drifting clouds in reflections, distance fog.
- Rain makes exposed ground wet and glossy, with puddles.
- The vanilla image takes over smoothly at the edge of the RT range.

### Fixes

- DLSS Ultra Quality (no longer supported by DLSS 310) runs as Quality at 77% scale instead of freezing the picture.

### Known issues

- Brightness and colours are not tuned yet.
- Indirect light is noisy without Ray Reconstruction; more rays per pixel smooth it.
- Ray Reconstruction may still freeze the picture; test RT without it first.

### Install

Replace the old JAR in `mods` (Fabric Loader 0.19.5+, Fabric API, Vulkan backend).
